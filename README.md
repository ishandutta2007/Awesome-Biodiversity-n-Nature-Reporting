# Awesome-Biodiversity-n-Nature-Reporting

## Top Biodiversity & Nature Reporting Ecosystem



**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**  

*Focused on Nature Intelligence, Ecosystem Monitoring & TNFD Compliance*  

**Last updated: September 2026**



This repository tracks notable **SaaS platforms** and **open-source projects** for **Biodiversity & Nature Reporting**. These tools measure, monitor, and report on ecosystem condition, species populations, and nature-related risks to support corporate disclosure frameworks like TNFD, CSRD, and SBTN.



**Examples** include NatureMetrics, NatureAlpha, GIST Impact, Impact Observatory, Map of Life, Plan A, NatureQuant, Climate Engine, Global Forest Watch Pro, and Planet Insights (the category leaders).



**Open-source emphasis**: This section is expanded with active projects for self-hosting, custom biodiversity indicator calculation, and transparent nature data processing — ideal for researchers, NGOs, and organizations building vendor-independent nature reporting pipelines. The open-source ecosystem offers strong foundations in satellite-based monitoring, ecosystem accounting, and indicator computation.



Contributions welcome! Open a PR to add/update entries. Keep descriptions factual and link to official sites.

## SaaS/Hosted Platforms



- **[NatureMetrics](https://naturemetrics.com/)**  

  The world's largest commercial provider of eDNA-based biodiversity monitoring, with 600+ companies in 126 countries using its Nature Intelligence Platform. Combines eDNA and bioacoustics sampling with AI-native screening to deliver TNFD, CSRD, and SBTN-aligned metrics. Features Nature Risk scores (0-1) per site, Ecosystem Condition Indices, and species-level insights validated against 4.3M+ detections .



- **[NatureAlpha](https://naturealpha.com/)**  

  Nature and biodiversity analytics platform for financial institutions, providing nature-related risk metrics and portfolio alignment assessments.



- **[GIST Impact](https://gistimpact.com/)**  

  Impact valuation platform quantifying corporate impacts on natural capital and biodiversity for reporting and decision-making.



- **[Impact Observatory](https://impactobservatory.com/)**  

  AI-powered geospatial analytics for land use, biodiversity, and ecosystem monitoring based on satellite imagery.



- **[Map of Life](https://mol.org/)**  

  Global biodiversity mapping platform integrating species distribution data for conservation planning and reporting.



- **[Plan A](https://plana.earth/)**  

  Carbon accounting and ESG management software with biodiversity and nature reporting capabilities.



- **[NatureQuant](https://naturequant.com/)**  

  Nature exposure quantification platform measuring the impact of natural environments on health and well-being.



- **[Climate Engine](https://climateengine.com/)**  

  Cloud-based geospatial analytics platform for environmental monitoring, drought assessment, and land management.



- **[Global Forest Watch Pro](https://pro.globalforestwatch.org/)**  

  WRI's platform for companies to monitor deforestation risk in supply chains and assess land use change impacts .



- **[Planet Insights](https://www.planet.com/)**  

  Satellite imagery and analytics platform providing monitoring for biodiversity, land use, and ecosystem change.



## Open-Source GitHub Projects



- **[BON in a Box](https://github.com/GEO-BON/bon-in-a-box-pipeline-engine)**  

  Open-source platform from the Group on Earth Observations Biodiversity Observation Network (GEO BON) providing open, transparent, and reproducible analysis pipelines that convert data into essential biodiversity variables and indicators. Built to help countries track progress toward the Kunming-Montreal Global Biodiversity Framework. Pipelines follow FAIR principles and are contributed by the expert community. Addresses fragmented monitoring efforts and capacity bottlenecks in national biodiversity reporting .



- **[Geo Engine / BioIS](https://github.com/geo-engine)**  

  Open-source geo-processing toolkit with native support for time-series, data connectors to Copernicus Dataspace Ecosystem, and an ML operator for incorporating trained models. The Biodiversity Indicator Service (BioIS) provides a user-facing application for discovering, auditing, and accessing biodiversity indicators computed on demand for uploaded company properties. Supports satellite-based monitoring of land usage and dominant tree species. Fully auditable declarative processing .



- **[Open Foris](https://www.openforis.org/)**  

  FAO's free, open-source initiative for forest and land monitoring, organized into five functional areas: field data collection (Arena Mobile, Ground), visual interpretation (Collect Earth Online, Collect Earth), data management (Collect, Arena), geospatial analysis (SEPAL), and integrated solutions. In 2025, Arena reached 3,883 users across 110 countries with 4,400+ mobile installations. Collect Earth Online supported interpretation of over 5.3 million samples with 16,000 active users. Designed for national forest monitoring, biodiversity assessments, and MRV for climate action .



- **[ARIES for SEEA](https://github.com/ARIES-IntegratedModelling)**  

  Open-source AI-powered platform for rapid natural capital accounting consistent with the SEEA Ecosystem Accounting framework. Uses semantics and machine reasoning to automate data and model integration. Generates ecosystem accounts for any user-specified terrestrial area globally using freely available remote-sensing data. Covers ecosystem extent (IUCN Global Ecosystem Typology), condition (forest types), and selected ecosystem services. Available via web browser or locally downloaded k.LAB software on the UN Global Platform .



- **[Deforisk](https://github.com/FAO-AIM4Forests/Deforisk)**  

  Open-source solution from FAO's AIM4Forests initiative generating deforestation risk maps using five modeling approaches. Supports evidence-based decision-making and policy action for governments and organizations. Version 2 allows users to input custom data for tailored risk models with enhanced tutorials. Integrated with national forest monitoring systems for risk assessment and carbon accounting .



- **[ecorisk](https://github.com/HeleneGutte/ecorisk)**  

  Open-source R package implementing a modular framework for ecosystem risk assessments. Combines expert-based and model-based pathways for semi-quantitative and quantitative analyses. Functions for exposure scoring (with uncertainty), sensitivity calculation (trait-based or general), vulnerability computation, and risk aggregation across multiple state indicators and pressures. Supports visualization with radar and heatmap plots. Published in CRAN with MIT license and companion paper in SoftwareX .



- **[biospat](https://github.com/anytko/biospat)**  

  Python package for Biodiversity Spatial Analysis. Characterizes ecosystem function, traits, and diversity of populations across a species' range. Identifies leading edges, trailing edges, and core distributions. Supports visualization at multiple spatial scales. MIT licensed .



- **[Biodiversity Estimation using MODIS NDVI & Land Cover](https://github.com/TEJASCHAVAN22/-Biodiversity-Estimation-Using-MODIS-NDVI-Land-Cover-2023-)**  

  Open-source workflow estimating biodiversity using MODIS NDVI and Land Cover data from Google Earth Engine. Calculates Shannon and Simpson diversity indices as proxies for ecological diversity, and visualizes vegetation heterogeneity using NDVI standard deviation. Adaptable for any area of interest and time period. Demonstrates remote-sensing-based biodiversity proxy assessment .



### Additional Strong Open-Source Options



- **ESG Biodiversity Toolkit** — Free, open-access digital solution from the BIOTRAILS project supporting companies integrating biodiversity into ESG reporting. Aligned with ESRS and TNFD sector-specific indicators. Covers five biodiversity impact drivers: climate change, land use change, pollution, resource exploitation, and biodiversity loss. Automatically calculates quantitative impact results .

- **NatureAlign** — Open-access application from NatureFinance helping financial institutions assess alignment with nature-positive outcomes through the Kunming-Montreal GBF lens. Uses biophysical, spatial, and financial datasets to analyze investments and loans against nature dependencies and impacts .

- **Dunya Analytics Free Tool** — Free biodiversity and nature risk analytics tool providing TNFD LEAP-aligned automated screening. Integrates IBAT datasets (IUCN Red List, WDPCA, WDKBA, STAR metric) and the Natural History Museum's Biodiversity Intactness Index. Lowers cost barriers for nature risk assessment .



**Frameworks for building custom nature reporting solutions**: Combine **BON in a Box** for reproducible indicator pipelines aligned with GBF monitoring frameworks . Use **Geo Engine / BioIS** for satellite-based biodiversity indicators with full auditability . Deploy **Open Foris** for field data collection, satellite interpretation, and national forest monitoring . Leverage **ARIES for SEEA** for ecosystem accounting consistent with UN standards . Use **ecorisk** for ecosystem risk assessment with uncertainty quantification . Note that commercial platforms like NatureMetrics provide eDNA-based species-level ground truth and validated detections that satellite-based open-source tools cannot replicate .

## Disclaimer



- This is a **community-curated** list — not exhaustive and not an endorsement.

- Biodiversity reporting tools must comply with applicable frameworks (TNFD, CSRD ESRS E4, SBTN, GBF) and regional regulations.

- Self-hosted open-source solutions require proper infrastructure, geospatial expertise, and ongoing data maintenance. Satellite-based indicators complement but do not replace ground-truth species data for compliance-grade reporting.

- The open-source ecosystem provides strong satellite monitoring, ecosystem accounting, and indicator calculation foundations, but commercial eDNA and bioacoustics platforms offer species-level validation that remains primarily a commercial offering.
